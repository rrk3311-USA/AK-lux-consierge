Summary

The recipe, in the open

The first three steps decide the quality of everything after them.

Proactive, with guardrails

The hustler is aggressive about finding opportunities, and careful about sending.

Approvals first. Outreach to new prospects is set to Ask until you raise it.
Email by default. Every message carries a working opt-out and your postal address, and opt-outs are honored promptly.
No cold texts without consent. Texting marketing messages needs prior written consent, so SMS stays off unless you have it.
Spend caps. Searches are cheap, but enrichment spends credits. A monthly cap stops it at the limit.
Suppression list. Anyone who opts out, bounces or replies "stop" is never contacted again.

General guidance, not legal advice. Have counsel review your outreach before you send at scale.
