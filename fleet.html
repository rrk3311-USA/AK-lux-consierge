The fleet

Every workflow is a ship.

Real fleets are built from classes of ship, each made for one job. Your automations are too. Pick the class for the job, add it to your fleet, and the Admiral keeps it battle ready.

Open the Bridge

Formations

Task force

A group of ships working toward one goal. Your Sales Task Force might be a frigate that catches leads, an oiler that enriches them and a cruiser that follows up.

Group of workflows

Carrier group

A carrier with its escorts. The carrier launches a campaign, and every other ship plays its part, then they stand down together.

One campaign, many ships

Convoy

Ships moving together under escort. A follow up sequence with an approval gate and a stop list so nothing sails into trouble.

Safe, ordered sequence

Example: the holiday carrier group

For a seasonal push such as Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday, one carrier launches the campaign and five escorts carry it out. Set its dates with the duration selector on the Hustler page.

Rules of engagement

Every ship sails under rules of engagement. They decide how much it may do on its own, borrowing the language of naval weapons control. New fleets start on Hold.
