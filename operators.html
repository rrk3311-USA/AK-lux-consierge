Your captain

Concierge-grade, by a real person.

The AI handles the volume. Your captain brings taste, judgment and a personal touch. We look for people who have spent their careers making premium customers feel looked after.

Loyalty and lifetime valuePremium fashionEntertainmentConsultative sales

The profile

A customer-focused professional with a background in:

  • Loyalty and lifetime value. Has built programs and habits that keep customers coming back, and knows how to measure it.
  • Premium brands. Fashion and entertainment experience, where presentation and tone are part of the product.
  • Consultative selling. Asks before pitching, and recommends what actually fits.
  • Product presentation. Can explain something technical so it feels simple and desirable.
  • Relationship building. Remembers people, follows up, and earns trust over time.
  • Personalized service. Treats each account as its own, not a ticket in a queue.

Why this background

The work is more concierge than technician.

  • Outbound has to sound human. Captains review every message the AI drafts for tone, taste and fit before it reaches a real person.
  • Retention is the business. For real estate agents, repeat and referral clients are lifetime value. Captains run the past-client plays that protect it.
  • Exceptions need judgment. When something is unusual or sensitive, a calm, well-spoken person makes the call with you.
  • Setup is a consultation. The first call is about understanding your business, not configuring software.

What a captain does

01

Onboarding consult

One short call to learn your clients, your tone and your rules, then builds your first setup with you.

02

Reviews the AI's work

Checks outreach drafts and unusual fixes. Approves, edits or sends back with a note.

03

Handles exceptions

Takes anything risky or unclear, and brings you a recommendation, not a question.

04

Protects your clients

Runs past-client check-ins, anniversaries and review requests so your best relationships stay warm.

05

Keeps you informed

Sends a short monthly check-in on what was done, what it earned and what to try next.
