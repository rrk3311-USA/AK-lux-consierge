Bridge · preview

Your whole business, one pocket.

Toggles turn things on or off. Health bars show whether an automation is working or broken. The Admiral fixes problems on its own, and you just read the history.

  • Folder tabs group everything: Design, Fleet, Outreach, Notion, Website, Rules.
  • Toggle on the right turns a file live or paused.
  • Health bar on each automation: green is healthy, yellow is degraded, red is broken. Sort by health from the top.
  • Callout at the top says how many need attention and that the Admiral is already on it. Tap it for the maintenance history.
  • Radar. A live picture of the whole fleet: healthy ships hug the center, struggling ones drift outward, and each squadron has its own color.
  • Your fleet. Each automation is a ship with a hull number, a home port and a run counter. Flip its guarded switch from IN PORT to UNDERWAY to put it to sea.
  • Enter icon in the middle opens the editor.
  • Views along the bottom: Files, Workflow Map, Operations Grid, Agent Control and the Intelligence Brief.
