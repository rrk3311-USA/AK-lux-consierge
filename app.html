Butler app · preview

Your whole business, one pocket.

Toggles turn things on or off. Health bars show whether an automation is working or broken. The butler fixes problems on its own, and you just read the history.

  • Folder tabs group everything: Design, Workflows, Outreach, Notion, Website, Rules.
  • Toggle on the right turns a file live or paused.
  • Health bar on each automation: green is healthy, yellow is degraded, red is broken. Sort by health from the top.
  • Callout at the top says how many need attention and that the butler is already on it. Tap it for the maintenance history.
  • Enter icon in the middle opens the editor.
  • Views along the bottom: Files, Workflow Map, Operations Grid, Agent Control and the Intelligence Brief.
