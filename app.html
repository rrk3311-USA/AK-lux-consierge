Your whole business, one pocket.

Every asset and workflow lives in a folder tab. Flip a toggle to turn it on or off. Tap the enter icon to edit it.

  • Folder tabs group things: Design, Workflows, Notion, Website, Rules.
  • Toggle on the right turns a file live or paused.
  • Enter icon in the middle opens the editor.
  • Changes save in this browser while we build the real backend.
