Connect once. The butler takes it from there.

FAQ

Straight answers

Is this another automation tool?

No. Keep the tools you already use. Agent Kammer sits above them and manages the workflows you built there.

Will it change things without asking?

Only inside rules you set. It starts read-only, and sensitive actions always wait for your approval. Every change is logged.

How does it reduce management?

It handles the routine babysitting: failures, retries, cost checks, documentation. You get one short briefing instead of many dashboards.

What about the website and launch kit?

They're the move-in kit: a logo, leather stationery, Notion hubs and a website the butler runs. Real estate agents get listing-ready sites out of the box.

Is my data safe?

Access is scoped per connection, with an audit trail. Security details will be published before general launch.
