FAQ

Straight answers

Do I need to be a real estate agent?

No. Real estate is our first playbook, but the same agents and launch kit work for any service business or boutique.

Who owns the site and the assets?

You do. Your logo files, stationery templates and Notion hubs are yours to keep, even if you cancel.

What does “agentic” mean here?

Instead of giving you a builder to learn, Kammer agents do the ongoing work: updates, SEO, content and lead handling. You approve changes or let them run on autopilot.

How are you cheaper than luxury agencies?

Agents handle the repeatable work that agencies bill hours for. You keep the taste and the polish, minus the retainer.

Can I get the stationery printed?

Yes. Templates are print-ready, and the Pro plan includes artwork files for your local printer or leather goods maker.
