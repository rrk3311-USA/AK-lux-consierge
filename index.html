  1. 01
    The hook

    Start with the pain, not the app.

    Every high-net-worth practice spends 10 to 15 hours a week on low-value, high-friction requests: hard reservations, private travel, lifestyle assets. Senior advisors end up as uncompensated personal assistants.
  2. 02
    The solution

    An internal-first force multiplier.

    One clean desk for lifestyle requests, vendors and exclusive access. Junior associates clear a request in minutes instead of untangling texts and email.
  3. 03
    The so-what

    Retention, and a magnet for new families.

    A front-facing curation layer shows architecture, prime real estate narratives and elite experiences. Prospects self-filter by taste before they book a discovery call.

    Answer compliance before it is asked.

    Strictly operational and lifestyle. No planning algorithms, no investment recommendations, no regulatory crossover.
