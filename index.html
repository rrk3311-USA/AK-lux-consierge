FAQ

Straight answers

Is a real person involved?

Yes. Every account has a human captain who sets things up with you, reviews the Admiral\u2019s work and handles exceptions. The AI does the routine volume so one captain can look after many accounts.

What do I actually have to do?

Customize a little at the start, then manage toggles in the app. Anything that needs your decision shows up as one tap.

Is this another automation tool?

No. Keep the tools you already use. Admiral Kammer sits above them and manages the workflows you built there.

Will it change things without asking?

Only inside rules you set. It starts read-only, and sensitive actions always wait for your approval. Every change is logged.

How does it reduce management?

It handles the routine babysitting: failures, retries, cost checks, documentation. You get one short briefing instead of many dashboards.

What about the website and launch kit?

They're the move-in kit: a logo, leather stationery, Notion hubs and a website the Admiral runs. Real estate agents get listing-ready sites out of the box.

Is my data safe?

Access is scoped per connection, with an audit trail. Security details will be published before general launch.
