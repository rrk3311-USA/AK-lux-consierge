# SOP Wiki

## SOP 1: Workflow fails
1. Admiral retries up to 3 times and logs it in the Run Log.
2. If still failing, it pauses the workflow and notes the cause in the Registry.
3. You get one line in the briefing. Approve the fix or reassign the owner.

## SOP 2: New workflow request
1. Describe it in one sentence to the Admiral.
2. Admiral drafts it and adds it to the Registry as Draft.
3. Owner reviews, approves, and it goes live.

## SOP 3: Monthly cost review
1. Open the Registry sorted by Monthly Cost.
2. Accept or decline the Admiral's merge and trim proposals.
3. Update the spend cap in Approvals & Rules.

## SOP 4: Someone leaves the team
1. Filter the Registry by their name.
2. Reassign each workflow owner.
3. Rotate connections they created.

## SOP 5: Outreach
1. Admiral researches the niche and refreshes the calibration file.
2. It finds and enriches prospects inside the credit cap and skips the suppression list.
3. Drafts land in your approval queue with the signal and source beside each.
4. Approve, edit or skip. Replies and opt-outs are logged; opt-outs are never contacted again.
