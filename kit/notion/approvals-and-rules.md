# Approvals & Standing Rules

> What the butler may do on its own, and what always needs your yes.

## Always ask first
- Sending messages to customers or prospects
- Anything touching money, billing or refunds
- Deleting workflows or data
- Connecting a new app or widening access

## Allowed automatically
- Retrying failed runs (max 3 attempts)
- Re-authenticating expired connections
- Pausing a workflow that is failing repeatedly
- Writing and updating runbooks
- Posting the daily briefing

- Escalate to: ___ (name / channel)
