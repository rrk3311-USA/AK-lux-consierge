Lead scoring & routing

Rules (demo): score = acquisition scale + timeline + breadth of interest + channel + detail. 70+ → Tier A to the senior advisor; 45–69 → Tier B to a private client associate; below 45 → editorial nurture. Tune the weights per practice.

Non-financial data separation

Two stores. One gate.

Lifestyle and prospect data live in separate stores. Only a consented contact and a tier cross the gate. Regulated data stays in the firm’s own systems.
